# Recovery Coloring Book Page Design System

This design system defines the production rules for the interior pages across the African American, Hispanic, and Multiracial product lines.

## Document setup
- Trim size: 8.5 x 11 in portrait.
- Bleed: 0.125 in only when needed for print; avoid full-bleed interiors when possible.
- Safe margin: 0.375 in minimum on all sides.
- Export: 300 DPI PNG and print-ready PDF.
- Interior color treatment: black line art on white background.

## Line system
- Primary contours: 0.75–1.25 pt.
- Secondary details: 0.5–0.75 pt.
- Use at least two visibly distinct line weights for hierarchy.
- Keep open coloring spaces; do not over-pack texture.
- Avoid gray shading except for proofing; final interiors should stay crisp black and white.

## Page types
- Solo portrait page: one clear focal person, emotional calm, light scene context.
- Community page: 2–5 people max, readable gestures, supportive interaction.
- Quote page: short affirmation only, centered composition, seasonal border accents.
- Environment page: restful room or outdoor space with strong colorable objects.
- Symbol page: motifs grouped in balanced clusters, not mandala overload.

## Trauma-informed rules
- Tone must feel calm, hopeful, reflective, grounded, and supportive.
- Exclude violence, drug use, visible injuries, panic, restraints, police scenes, and weapons.
- Body language should be soft and open; no extreme distress cues.
- Alternate quiet pages and social pages for emotional pacing.

## Representation
- African American line: natural hairstyles, broad tone range, everyday fashion, respectful home/community settings.
- Hispanic line: varied skin tones, warm family scenes, optional bilingual quotes, culturally familiar domestic details.
- Multiracial line: visibly diverse tones, mixed-feature faces, blended family and friend groups.
- Across all lines: no stereotypes, no exaggerated costume cues, no tokenized representation.

## Seasonal system
- Spring: flowers, windows, rain, growth, fresh-start mood.
- Summer: sunlight, porches, parks, gardens, open-air calm.
- Fall: leaves, tea, blankets, candles, gratitude.
- Winter: quilts, snow, cocoa, lamps, indoor peace.

## Export QA
- Verify text placement inside safe margins.
- Verify all lines remain above 0.5 pt.
- Test at home-printer scale and on-screen PDF view.
- Check quote spelling, punctuation, and accents.
- Final deliverables: source art, PNG page, and compiled PDF book interior.
